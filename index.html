Our Story

From Bean to Cup,
With Care

What started as a small passion project in Portland's Pearl District has grown into a beloved community gathering spot. At The Daily Grind, we believe every cup tells a story — from the farmer who cultivated the beans to the barista who perfected your pour.

We source directly from small farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, ensuring fair wages and exceptional quality in every bag.

Single Origin

Beans sourced directly from premier farms around the world, each with unique flavor profiles.

Freshly Roasted

Roasted in small batches every morning to ensure peak freshness and bold, complex flavors.
